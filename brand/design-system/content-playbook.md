# Content playbook

**Classification: INTERNAL.** Every topic below still needs a research session in Fieldnote before any science claim is made. The Level bukti comes from that research, not from this list.

## Five series

Each series has a fixed name and a fixed shape, so followers recognise it in the feed. Use the series name as the first `mono-label` line on images and as the video's on-screen tag.

| Series | Shape | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cek Kandungan** | One ingredient: what it is (with an analogy), what it does, who it suits, what to pair or avoid | "Fluoride di pasta gigi: kerjanya gimana?" |
| **Mitos atau Riset?** | A viral claim → what studies actually tested → verdict with its limit | "Biotin bikin rambut tebal?" |
| **Kata Studi** | One study retold: who, how long, what changed, and what it does *not* show | "Studi ceramide: 4 minggu, kulit kering" |
| **Pilih yang Mana** | Two options side by side, with how to choose | "Deodoran vs antiperspiran" |
| **Rutin Masuk Akal** | Order of use, frequency, what not to stack | "Urutan pakai: serum dulu atau pelembap?" |

## Post shapes

**X thread (2–4 segments)**
1. Hook that works alone, with the thumbnail image.
2. The science in plain words, with the explainer image.
3. The research and its limit, or "how to choose".
4. The product as an example, plus "Link afiliasi: …".

**Threads post or carousel (3–5 slides)**
1. Hook.
2. The concept, as a diagram.
3. What research says, with its source line.
4. How to choose or use it.
5. The product as an example, plus the link note in the caption.

**TikTok / Shopee Video script (30–60s)**

| Time | Part | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Hook | A question or surprising fact on screen in `display-l` |
| 2–15s | The problem | A reader situation, never an invented personal story |
| 15–35s | The science | One analogy (tembok bata, tutup panci, lensa) |
| 35–50s | The research | Who/how long and the limit, shown as a `SourceLine` on screen |
| 50–60s | Example + CTA | "Contoh yang komposisinya begitu ada di keranjang kuning / link di bio. Itu link afiliasi ya." |

## Hook bank

- "Udah pakai ___ tapi ___ masih ___? Bisa jadi bukan produknya yang salah."
- "___ itu sebenarnya apa sih? Jelasinnya pakai ___."
- "Viral: ___. Aku cek studinya, ternyata…"
- "Bedanya ___ dan ___ cuma satu hal ini."
- "Label ___ di kemasan itu artinya apa?"

## Topic backlog by area

- **Rambut:** rontok vs patah, minyak rambut (argan, kemiri), kondisioner sebelum sampo, heat protectant, ketombe dan zinc pyrithione.
- **Kulit:** ceramide, niacinamide dan breakout, sunscreen kimia vs fisik, double cleansing, retinol untuk pemula.
- **Gigi & mulut:** fluoride, obat kumur dengan alkohol, sikat elektrik vs manual, pasta gigi pemutih, benang gigi.
- **Mata:** mata lelah dan layar, tetes mata "whitening" (ingredient info only, check rules first), riasan mata dan kebersihan kuas, softlens dan kebersihan.
- **Badan & kuku:** deodoran vs antiperspiran, kuku rapuh, lotion untuk kulit bersisik, sabun antibakteri sehari-hari.
