## One research → every platform

1. **Fieldnote research session.** Find and approve the evidence. Approval sets the Level bukti.
2. **Fieldnote writing session.** Produces the X thread and Threads post.
3. **Post images.** Use the `fieldnote-post-image` skill: a thumbnail plus one explainer.
4. **TikTok script.** Follow the structure in the Content playbook, using the same facts.
5. **Shopee.** Upload the same video as Shopee Video, and add the product to the area collection with its one-line reason.
6. **Instagram and Facebook.** Reuse the carousel and Reel.

## Disclosure and safety

- Write "Link afiliasi" in the same post or segment as every affiliate link, on every platform.
- Platform toggles: TikTok and Instagram have "paid partnership" or branded-content toggles for sponsored deals. Whether plain affiliate links need them is *unverified*, so check each platform's current policy.
- Eye and dental content: "Bukan saran medis", no diagnosis, no prescription treatment. Check Indonesian cosmetic and health advertising rules (BPOM) before promoting eye or dental products (*unverified*).
- Medical questions in comments or DMs: reply with the template below, never with a diagnosis.

> Aku bukan dokter, jadi nggak bisa menilai kondisi kamu dari sini. Untuk keluhan seperti ini, paling aman cek ke dokter (atau dokter gigi/mata). Yang bisa aku bantu: penjelasan umum soal kandungan produknya.

## Community

- Answer real questions with the source and its limitation. Turn repeated questions into the next topic.
- Correct mistakes publicly: edit the post or reply with "Koreksi: …" and the right source. It builds trust faster than staying silent.
- Don't argue with brands or creators by name. Compare claims, not people.
