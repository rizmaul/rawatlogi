The Rawatlogi wordmark, lens mark, profile avatar and "rl" monogram.

- **Wordmark:** `rawatlogi`, lowercase, Plus Jakarta Sans 800, tracking -0.035em. "rawat" is set in `ink`, "logi" in `teal`. Never retype it in another face, never split it onto two lines, never write "RawatLogi".
- **Lens mark:** a `teal` ring (stroke about 18% of its diameter), an `ink` handle at 45° to the lower right, and a small `coral` dot inside the ring. It stands for "zooming in on a claim until its research shows".
- **Lockup:** the lens mark sits left of the wordmark, its height about 1.1× the wordmark's x-height to cap height, with a gap of `space-2` to `space-3`.
- **Avatar:** the lens on an `ink` circle, with the ring in light teal (#3fb8a8) and the handle in `paper`. Use it for every profile photo.
- **Monogram:** "rl" in `paper` on a `teal` square with `radius-lg` corners. Use it only where the avatar would be smaller than about 40px (shop icons, favicons).
- **On images:** the wordmark alone, about 28–30px tall on 1600-wide images, in a bottom corner inside the `space-8` safe margin.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the "r" on all sides. Never place it on a busy area or on an area colour.
