The citation line under any research claim or chart: who, how many, how long, and the main limitation.

- **Consumer provides:** the first author and year from the approved evidence, the population or sample size if recorded, the duration, and one short limitation in Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Format:** `Sumber: <Penulis> et al., <tahun> · n = <jumlah> · <durasi>` on one line in `mono-source`, `teal`. The limitation goes below it in `body-sm`, `muted`, starting with "Batasan:".
- **Rules:**
  - Never invent a number. Leave out a part that isn't recorded.
  - Required on every image that shows a study result or chart.
  - Not used on "Ilustrasi konsep" images, since they make no study claim.
