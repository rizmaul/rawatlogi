The Level bukti pill, Rawatlogi's signature: it tells the reader how strong the research behind a post is.

- **Consumer provides:** the level, taken from Rawatlogi's evidence status (never from the product listing), and the size (`label` style).
- **Variants:**
  - **Riset kuat:** fill `evidence-strong`, text `on-evidence-strong`. Only for approved evidence from systematic reviews or several consistent trials.
  - **Riset terbatas:** 3px solid `evidence-strong` outline, `teal` text. For any other approved evidence.
  - **Ilustrasi konsep:** 3px dashed `evidence-concept` outline and text. For general-science diagrams with no approved evidence.
  - **Belum ada riset:** fill `area-kulit`, `ink` text. When the point is that research is missing.
- **Placement:** after the `AreaLabel` in a top corner of every image; on TikTok and Shopee videos, on screen for the whole video.
- **Rule:** when unsure between two levels, choose the lower one. A post with no approved evidence can never carry "Riset kuat" or "Riset terbatas".
